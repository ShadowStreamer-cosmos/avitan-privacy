# AVITAN — Data Deletion

AVITAN is a local-first app. Your data stays on your device, and you
control it. This page explains how to delete it.

## On-device data (the vast majority)

- **Wipe everything instantly**: Settings → Data & privacy → Privacy &
  memory → "Wipe all data". This deletes every entry and resets the app
  (works offline, irreversible).
- **Delete individual items**: delete any chat, note, health reading,
  transaction, or stored memory fact at any time from within the app.
- **Memory facts**: Chat → AI chat → "What I remember" — correct, forget
  or mute any stored fact.
- **Deleting the app** removes all remaining on-device data.

## Server-side data (only what you explicitly share)

- **Share links** (caregiver/doctor summaries you create): tap **Revoke**
  in the app to delete the stored summary immediately; otherwise it
  auto-expires after 90 days.
- **Analytics digests** (only if you opted in): event counts only, no
  personal data, deleted after 30 days.
- **Purchase records**: subscription state (purchase token + expiry) kept
  only to maintain your license — no personal data.

## Requesting deletion

If you cannot perform the steps above (for example, you lost access to
your device), email us at the support address shown in the Google Play
Store listing with "Data deletion request" in the subject line, and we
will remove any associated server-side data within 30 days.
